Module 2

The 5 Vs of Big Data

Use the 5 Vs to understand what makes Big Data different from ordinary data.

Learning Objectives

  • Describe each of the 5 Vs.
  • Connect each V to a realistic data example.
  • Explain how the 5 Vs help determine whether data is useful.

Lesson Content

The 5 Vs are a simple way to describe Big Data. They help explain why some data is harder to manage and why special tools or careful planning may be needed.

Volume

The amount of data, such as billions of records.

Velocity

How quickly data is created, shared, or updated.

Variety

Different forms of data, including text, video, tables, and audio.

Veracity

How accurate, trustworthy, or messy the data is.

Value

The useful insight or decision that can come from the data.

For example, a social media platform has volume because it stores huge amounts of posts, velocity because new posts appear every second, and variety because users share text, photos, videos, reactions, and comments.

Interactive Activity

Prototype activity: students match examples to the correct V.

  • "New messages arrive every second" matches Velocity.
  • "Some reviews are fake or unclear" matches Veracity.
  • "A company uses data to improve delivery routes" matches Value.

Knowledge Check

Question: Which V is about whether data can be trusted?

Answer: Veracity.
