About My Role

My role in this project is Project Manager, CEO, and Developer, which basically means I am trying to keep the group moving while also building a lot of the actual website. For me, this project is a chance to practice being organized instead of just jumping straight into code. I need to keep track of what pages we have, what still needs content, what should be committed to GitHub, and how the work can be split so everyone has something useful to do.

On the developer side, my main strength is that I can move fast and figure things out by testing, but that can also become a weakness if I do too much at once. So for this prototype, I am trying to work in smaller steps: first the homepage, then the module pages, then the team pages, then login and register pages, and then simple interactions. I also want the code to stay readable because this is a group project, not just something one person understands.

Another part of my job is managing GitHub. That means making commits that show the project was built step by step and helping teammates know how to pull, edit, and push their work. I am still improving at planning ahead, but I think this role fits me because I like connecting the technical side with the team side. My goal is for the final project to look organized, useful, and clearly made by our group.
