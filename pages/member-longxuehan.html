About My Role

My role is to guide the learning experience from start to finish. I help connect the course goals, lesson sequence, activities, and team contributions so the site feels organized instead of scattered.

For learners, my focus is flow. The homepage should make the path obvious, the modules should build in a clear order, and every button should help the learner know where to go next. Good structure makes Big Data feel less overwhelming.

I also help keep the tone consistent across the course. My goal is for the final experience to feel useful, complete, and easy to present: a Big Data introduction that students can actually move through with confidence.
