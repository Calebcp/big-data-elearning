About My Role

My role is Creative Leader and Designer, so I focus on how the course looks and feels to use. Big Data can become dry if it is only text, so the design needs to make each page clear, modern, and easy to scan.

I think about color, spacing, cards, buttons, images, and the way learners move from one section to the next. The goal is to make the course feel energetic without making the interface confusing.

I also help shape visual examples for ideas like the 5 Vs and the data pipeline. My goal is for the site to feel polished enough for a presentation and friendly enough that students want to keep clicking through it.
