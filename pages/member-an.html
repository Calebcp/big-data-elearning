About My Role

My role is Audio/Music and Multimedia. I help make the course feel more interactive and less like a plain document. Big Data can sound complicated at first, so media helps make the topic easier to approach.

I focus on useful media: short audio moments, subtle feedback cues, and simple visuals that support the activities. Media should make the learner feel more engaged without taking attention away from the lesson.

My goal is to make the course more memorable. When a learner answers a question or follows a visual explanation, the feedback should feel clear, quick, and connected to the Big Data idea being taught.
