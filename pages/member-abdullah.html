About My Role

My role is CIO and Writer, which means I am working with the information side of the project. The website is about Big Data, so the writing cannot just be random facts copied onto a page. It needs to explain ideas in a way that a beginner can follow. I need to help organize the module content, choose examples, and make sure each page teaches something useful without becoming too long or boring.

As Writer, I am responsible for lesson explanations, vocabulary, short examples, quiz questions, and activity instructions. One strength I can bring is helping the site sound more like an actual learning tool instead of just a coding demo. At the same time, one weakness I need to watch is writing too much. If the text becomes a huge block, students will probably skip it. So I want to use headings, bullet points, short paragraphs, and simple language.

The CIO part also means helping keep information organized for the team. That can include content outlines, testing notes, and making sure the final quiz matches what was actually taught in the modules. My goal is to make the learning path feel clear: first students understand what Big Data is, then they learn the 5 Vs, then types of data, then the pipeline, and finally real-world uses and challenges. If the content is clear, the whole project becomes stronger.
