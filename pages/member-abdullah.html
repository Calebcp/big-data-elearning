About My Role

My role is CIO and Writer, which means I focus on the information learners need. Big Data can feel abstract, so the writing needs to explain each idea in a way that a beginner can follow.

I help shape lesson explanations, vocabulary, short examples, quiz questions, and activity instructions. The goal is to keep the text clear and useful without turning each page into a long block of reading.

My goal is to keep the learning path clear: first students understand what Big Data is, then they learn the 5 Vs, then types of data, then the pipeline, and finally real-world uses and challenges. Clear content makes the whole course stronger.
