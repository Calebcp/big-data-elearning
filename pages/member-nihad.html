About My Role

My role is CTO and Tester, so I am looking at the project from both a technical side and a user side. As CTO, I help think about whether the website structure makes sense and whether the team is using the right level of technology for this class. Since we are using HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, the challenge is not to make the project overly complicated. The challenge is to make it clean, understandable, and easy for the whole team to work on.

As Tester, I need to be the person who notices problems before the project is submitted. That means checking links, buttons, mobile layout, spelling, images, and whether the pages feel consistent. I also need to test the website like a normal student would use it. If a learner cannot find the next module, does not understand what an activity is asking, or gets confused by the layout, then that is something I should catch and report.

One strength for this role is being able to look carefully at details. A weakness I want to improve is explaining bugs clearly so the developer or designer knows what to fix. For this project, I want to make testing feel like part of the process, not just something we do at the end. If we test early, the site will be easier to improve and less stressful to finish.
