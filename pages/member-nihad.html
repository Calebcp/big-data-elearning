About My Role

My role is to review the course from a learner's point of view. I look for places where the path could be clearer, where an activity needs better feedback, or where a page should be easier to scan.

I check whether links, buttons, lesson sections, quiz questions, images, and mobile layouts feel consistent. If a learner cannot find the next module or does not understand what an activity is asking, that is a learning problem worth fixing.

My goal is to make the course dependable. Learners should be able to focus on Big Data concepts, not on figuring out how the website works.
